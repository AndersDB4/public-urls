# Terms of Use

Last updated: 8 September 2026

## Purpose and permitted use

This bank-mcp application is a personal, non-commercial setup operated by AndersDB4 to access and analyse the operator’s own bank account information. It is not offered as a banking service to other users.

## Read-only access

The application is intended to retrieve account information, balances, and transactions from accounts the operator is authorised to access. It does not provide functionality to initiate payments, transfer funds, or change bank account settings.

## Authorisation and third-party services

Bank access requires authorisation through Enable Banking and the relevant bank. Access is limited to the accounts and permissions authorised by the account holder. Enable Banking, the bank, and OpenAI remain subject to their own applicable terms and privacy notices; these terms do not replace them.

Financial information may be sent to OpenAI when the operator requests analysis through an AI assistant. The operator should review the applicable data controls before using this feature.

## Accuracy and use of results

Bank data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. AI-generated analysis may contain errors. The operator should verify balances, transactions, and any resulting conclusions against official bank records before relying on them. This application does not provide professional financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

## Stopping use

The operator may stop using the application at any time, disconnect it from the AI assistant, and revoke bank-data access using the controls made available by the bank or Enable Banking. Stopping access does not automatically delete information already stored by other services; their applicable policies and controls govern that information.

## Privacy and contact

The privacy notice is maintained in this repository alongside these terms. Questions about this personal setup may be raised through the repository’s Issues page. Do not include account numbers, credentials, financial records, or other private information in a public issue.
